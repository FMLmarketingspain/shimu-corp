Give a Classroom the Tools to Learn Better

Just $15 gives one classroom a whiteboard — clearer lessons and cleaner, dust-free air for the students and teachers who use it every day.

A blackboard can only do so much.

Chalk fades fast, breaks easily, and makes it hard for teachers to write clearly or for students at the back of the room to see. It slows lessons down and makes it harder for teachers to teach the way they want to. And every time an eraser hits that board, a fine cloud of chalk dust fills the air — dust that students and teachers breathe in, lesson after lesson, day after day.

A whiteboard changes that. Writing is bolder, cleaner, and easier to read from anywhere in the room. Teachers can explain ideas more clearly, correct mistakes without a cloud of dust, and keep lessons moving. For students, that means better focus, better understanding, and a classroom that actually works in their favour — and lungs that aren't working overtime just to get through the school day.

TASPO's Whiteboards for All project is bringing that upgrade to classrooms across Tanzania — one whiteboard at a time.

Just $15 gives one classroom a whiteboard.
Why It Matters

Small changes, big difference

Small changes in the classroom can make a big difference in how much a child actually learns — and how healthy they stay while learning it.

Clearer writing means clearer thinking

Bold, high-contrast whiteboard markers are easier to read than chalk — especially for students sitting further back or with mild vision difficulties that often go undiagnosed.

Cleaner air means healthier classrooms

Chalk dust is a known respiratory irritant. Repeated exposure can trigger coughing, sneezing, and irritated eyes, and it's a particular problem for children with asthma or other breathing sensitivities. Whiteboard markers remove that dust from the room entirely, giving students and teachers a cleaner space to breathe in every day.

Less time cleaning, more time teaching

Wiping a whiteboard takes seconds, without the dust cloud that chalk erasers kick up. That's more lesson time and less disruption, every single day.

A better tool for a better lesson

Teachers can write, revise, and diagram more freely on a whiteboard — making it easier to explain new ideas step by step, rather than working around a board that's hard to write on and even harder to erase cleanly.

A small upgrade, a lasting impact

A single whiteboard can serve a classroom for years, quietly improving how every lesson is delivered — and how much dust every child in that room breathes in — long after the initial donation.
